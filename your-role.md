# Your Role

Before you learn about what exactly this position offers, you need to understand what our goal as a company is. Your role will make a lot more sense.

Political campaigns collect signatures from the public to get political candidates or initiative questions on the ballot, which occurs at all scales, from local to state governments. The campaigns need to "validate" their signatures to ensure that the signatures are from registered voters, and to also have a list of everybody who signed in the event that the campaign gets "challenged" by an opposing campaign or party who wishes to disqualify their candidate/question from getting on the ballot.&#x20;

Currently, political campaigns validate all of their signatures manually, usually in-house, so in their own offices with their own staff, paid hourly. They do everything by hand: manually searching in a voter database for every signature on their petitions, then categorizing it and marking it as such.

Certifi is our solution to slow signature validation. It allows campaigns to remotely upload scans of their petition sheets to us, and we then validate them. We use AI with the ability to read handwriting to match each signature to a voter, but it's not perfect. This is where you come in.

As a validator, you will:

* Review images of petition signatures
* Search for the signer in a voter database by accurately querying what you see
* Categorize whether a signature is valid, invalid, or a myriad of other categorizations based on what you see and a set of rules
* Follow strict validation rules and internal guidelines
* Maintain both speed and high accuracy

Because you will be an independent contractor, you can flexibly work whenever you want. You will also need to fill out a W-9 form before beginning. You can log in and validate signatures whenever we have them. Project volume fluctuates based on how many clients we have at once, which is at least one client at all times, meaning at least a few thousand signatures per week. Our goal is to secure year-round, 24/7 validation work for you all, so there will never be a shortage of signatures to validate!&#x20;

Pay is task-based like other gig-work opportunities. For each signature you validate, you will earn money. There are different types of signatures you can validate (more on this soon), each with their own pay. The pay rates for each of these types of signatures also change for each project. Our last project paid 8 cents per signature; our current one pays 12. As you get faster and better at validating, you may be able to validate hundreds of signatures per hour. We pay weekly via Zelle or PayPal.

Your results will be audited. Your accuracy must meet our standards, as we have a large emphasis on accuracy.
