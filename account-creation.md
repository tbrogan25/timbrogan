# Account Creation

Before you can create your account, you need to send Tim or Patrick your:

* Full name
* Preferred email address

Then, they will manually invite you to create an account. You will receive an email from **support@certifiapp.com** that looks like this:

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (18) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="danger" %}
> **IMPORTANT: YOUR PASSWORD MUST MEET ALL REQUIREMENTS ON THE FIRST ATTEMPT, OR WE WILL NEED TO SEND YOU A NEW REGISTRATION EMAIL.**
>
> * 12+ Total Characters
> * Special Character
> * Lower Case Letter
> * Upper Case Letter
{% endhint %}

Enter your name, email address, password, and registration code. Ensure that your email is the same one you received the code with, ensure your password meets the requirements above, and enter your registration code that was emailed to you.

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (19) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Check your email for another code, which you will enter below:

{% hint style="danger" %}
If you don't see this email in your inbox, check your Spam folder.
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (20) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (21) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Then sign in with the same email and password you entered above. Make sure to select the **Validator** button:

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (23) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Perfect - you've now created your account. You will see your signatures to validate here when they are available.

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (25) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Once your account has been created, directly message Tim or Patrick about receiving first training turn-in.
