# Training

You will be trained using two real batches, or "turn-ins," of signatures.

## Turn-In #1

**Duration:** 100 signatures

**Deadline:** 3 days upon assignment

The purpose of this first turn-in is to gauge how much you enjoy the task of validating ("Can I see myself doing this for long periods of time?") as well as your initial accuracy.

The level of difficulty is easy — you won't have a hard time finding the correct matches.

We encourage you to ask your questions in our community Discord server, which is linked in the next article.

## Turn-In #2

**Duration:** 392 signatures

**Deadline:** 24 hours upon assignment

After you've completed your first turn-in on time and with a good accuracy rate, the purpose of this second turn-in is to gauge how you handle a large amount of signatures in a shorter time frame while maintaing your accuracy.

Again, ask any questions in the Discord server.&#x20;

Once you've successfully completed this turn-in, you'll have completed training.

Again, work in this industry is unstable, so there may not be any work readily available right when you've finished training.
