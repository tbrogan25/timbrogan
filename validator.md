# Your Role

Before understanding the position, it’s important to understand what we do. Your role will make a lot more sense.

Political campaigns must collect signatures from registered voters to place candidates or ballot questions on the ballot. These signatures must be **validated** to confirm they belong to registered voters. Campaigns must also maintain accurate records in case an opposing campaign challenges their petition and attempts to disqualify signatures.&#x20;

Traditionally, campaigns handle this process manually. Staff members sit in offices and search voter databases one signature at a time, categorizing each as valid or invalid by hand.

**Certifi modernizes this process.**

Campaigns upload scanned petition sheets to our platform, and we use AI to read handwriting and match signatures to voter records. Because AI is not perfect, human validators review and confirm the results.

That’s where you come in.

As a validator, you will:

* Read a handwritten name and address from a scanned image
* Visually match, or manually transcribe that information into a search box
* If a match appears in the search results, select it to mark the signature as valid
* Categorize signatures based on strict validation rules and internal guidelines
* Maintain high accuracy

All work is audited for accuracy. Because campaigns rely on our data, maintaining high accuracy is mandatory. Validators must meet minimum accuracy standards to remain active.

#### Work Structure

This is a 1099 independent contractor role. You will need to complete a W-9 form before beginning.

There are no fixed shifts. You may log in and work whenever signatures are available. Project volume fluctuates depending on client demand, but we always have at least one active client, which means thousands of signatures per week. Our goal is to secure year-round validation work so there is never a shortage of tasks.

#### Pay

This is task-based gig work rather than hourly employment. You are paid per signature validated. Different types of validation tasks may have different rates, and pay varies by project.

For example, our last project paid $0.08 per signature, and our current project pays $0.12 per signature. As you become faster and more accurate, it is possible to validate hundreds of signatures per hour.

Payments are issued weekly via Zelle or PayPal.

No special software is needed. All work is completed through your web browser.

Prior experience in data entry, auditing, compliance, or other typing-based roles is helpful for you but not required.
